Bio自述

I am Zhenfang Zhu (Chinese: 朱振方), a graduate of the University of Science and Technology of China. Today, I work on AI Agent Coding.

In the past, building software was expensive. You had to know how to code, find engineers, assemble a team, and spend months turning an idea into a product.

AI is compressing all of that. In the future, when an ordinary person has an idea, they should be able to build it directly.

I believe the most important capability of the next generation is not learning more tools. It is the courage to imagine, to act, and to create.

Code will become cheaper and cheaper.

Imagination will not.

I want to lower the barrier to creating software until only one question remains: What do you actually want to build?

I am increasingly unconvinced that reality happens only while we are awake.

We are used to calling everything that happens while we are awake “real”: studying, working, building companies, meeting, parting…; while the people we see, the places we reach, and the stories we live through after falling asleep are all classified as “dreams.”

But if an experience once made you afraid, happy, regretful, or confused, and you still remember it many years after waking, can it really be dismissed as unreal?

So I began to see life as two parallel worlds.

One begins when I open my eyes; the other begins when I close them.

What happens while I am awake and what happens after I fall asleep: both have truly passed through me.

The former leaves credentials, work, relationships, and time; the latter leaves memories, emotions, and traces that cannot be proven, but cannot be denied either.

I no longer try to decide which world is more real.

Perhaps what we call reality is only a dream we share, while what we call a dream is a reality remembered by one person alone.

That is why I made this website.

To keep, in one place, the selves I have been while awake and the worlds I have reached in sleep.

This is not my personal homepage.

For now, I am simply standing on this side and recording.

The next time I wake, I may be on the other side.

我越来越不相信，现实只发生在清醒的时候。

我们习惯把醒着时经历的一切称作“真实”：读书、工作、创业、相遇、离开…；而把睡着之后看到的人、抵达的地方、经历的故事… 统统归为“梦”。

可如果一段经历曾让你恐惧、快乐、遗憾、困惑，甚至在醒来很多年后依然记得它，那么它真的可以被简单地称为虚假吗？

于是我开始把人生看成两个彼此平行的世界。

一个发生在睁开眼睛之后，一个发生在闭上眼睛之后。

醒着时发生的，和睡着后发生的，它们都曾真实地经过我。

前者留下履历、作品、关系和时间；后者留下无法证明，却同样无法否认的记忆、情绪与痕迹。

我不再试图判断哪一个世界更加真实。

也许所谓现实，不过是我们共同做着的一场梦；而所谓梦境，只是一场只有自己记得的现实。

所以我做了这个网站。

把清醒时成为过的自己，和睡梦中抵达过的世界，放在同一个地方。

这里不是我的个人主页。

我只是此刻，站在这一边记录。

下一次醒来，我也许就在另一边。

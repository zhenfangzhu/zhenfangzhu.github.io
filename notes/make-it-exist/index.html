让它存在Make It Exist

我越来越觉得，做产品和记录梦，本质上是在做同一件事。

把原本不存在的东西，带到这个世界里。

一个想法原本不存在。

写成代码以后，它存在了。

一个梦醒来以后原本会消失。

写下来以后，它也存在了。

所以比起“创业者”“程序员”或者其他身份，我越来越喜欢一个更简单的词：

Creator.

我想看看，一个人究竟能让多少原本不存在的东西，来到这个世界。

I increasingly feel that building products and recording dreams are, at their core, the same thing.

Bringing something that did not exist into this world.

An idea does not exist at first.

Once written in code, it exists.

A dream would disappear after waking.

Once written down, it exists too.

So rather than “entrepreneur,” “programmer,” or any other identity, I increasingly prefer a simpler word:

Creator.

I want to see how many things that did not exist one person can bring into this world.

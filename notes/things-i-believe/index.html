🐷 赚钱第一性原理 🐷 First Principles of Wealth Creation

Notes · Things I believe随笔 · 我相信的事

赚钱的第一性原理是创造价值，不是出卖时间。

创造价值的第一性原理是解决问题，不是提供劳动。

解决问题的第一性原理是识别真需求，不是自我感动。

识别真需求的第一性原理是实战反馈，不是闭门造车。

实战反馈的第一性原理是快速迭代，不是仔细打磨。

快速迭代的第一性原理是验证模型，不是为了交付。

验证模型的第一性原理是为了系统复制，不是个人苦干。

系统复制的第一性原理是杠杆思维，不是线性积累。

📈 价值增长公式

$$\text{Total Wealth} = \frac{\text{Value} \times \text{Solving Problems}}{\text{Subjective Emotion}} \times (\text{Iteration})^{\text{Speed}} \times \text{Leverage}$$

⚒️ 变量解析：

  • 分子：你解决问题的深度和广度，这是业务“底座”。
  • 分母：主观偏见、自我执念、自我怜悯。分母越小，价值传导效率越高。
  • 指数：每一次非线性变量。速度越快，纠错成本越低，进化效率越高。
  • 系数：放大器。没有杠杆（代码、内容、资本），增长永远是线性的体力活。

🧩 模型分层

🟢 第一层：价值锚点
  • 核心指标：解决痛点的程度。
  • 避坑指南：我们需要警惕“低水平勤奋”，不能用“忙碌”来掩盖“无能”。
🟡 第二层：生存验证
  • 核心指标：反馈周期
  • 避坑指南：如果我们不为第一版产品感到难堪，说明我们发晚了，所以我们需要拒绝闭门造车，去向市场接触。
🔴 第三层：规模效应
  • 核心指标：系统化程度
  • 避坑指南：拒绝“个人苦干”，问自己：如果我睡着了，这个系统还能为我运转吗？

⚠️ 警示

  • 打磨陷阱：在未验证模型前进行“仔细打磨”，本质上是高成本的博弈。
  • 交付误区：交付只是手段，验证模型是否能自动运行才是目的。
  • 线性诅咒：任何无法进入“系统复制”层级的努力，终究会被时间耗死。

The first principle of making money is creating value, not selling time.

The first principle of creating value is solving problems, not providing labor.

The first principle of solving problems is identifying real demand, not self-indulgence.

The first principle of identifying real demand is real-world feedback, not building behind closed doors.

The first principle of real-world feedback is rapid iteration, not premature polishing.

The first principle of rapid iteration is validating models, not merely delivering.

The first principle of validating models is systematic replication, not solitary hard grind.

The first principle of systematic replication is leverage thinking, not linear accumulation.

📈 Value Growth Formula

$$\text{Total Wealth} = \frac{\text{Value} \times \text{Solving Problems}}{\text{Subjective Emotion}} \times (\text{Iteration})^{\text{Speed}} \times \text{Leverage}$$

⚒️ Variable Breakdown:

  • Numerator: The depth and breadth of problems solved—the bedrock of your business.
  • Denominator: Subjective bias, ego attachments, and self-pity. The smaller the denominator, the higher the value transmission efficiency.
  • Exponent: Non-linear acceleration. Faster speed means lower correction costs and higher evolutionary efficiency.
  • Coefficient: Amplifier. Without leverage (code, content, capital), growth remains a linear treadmill of manual labor.

🧩 Model Hierarchy

🟢 Level 1: Value Anchor
  • Core Metric: Degree of pain point resolution.
  • Pitfall Guide: Beware of "low-level diligence"—never use busyness to conceal incompetence.
🟡 Level 2: Survival Validation
  • Core Metric: Feedback cycle duration.
  • Pitfall Guide: If you aren't embarrassed by the first version of your product, you launched too late. Reject building in isolation; engage directly with the market.
🔴 Level 3: Economies of Scale
  • Core Metric: Degree of systematization.
  • Pitfall Guide: Reject solitary brute force. Ask yourself: if I am asleep, can this system continue running and generating value for me?

⚠️ Key Warnings

  • Polishing Trap: Polishing endlessly before validating the model is fundamentally a high-cost gamble.
  • Delivery Fallacy: Delivery is merely a means; validating whether the model can run autonomously is the true objective.
  • Linear Curse: Any effort that cannot ascend to systematic replication will ultimately be consumed by time.

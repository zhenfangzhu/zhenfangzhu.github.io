## About

I am an AI entrepreneur and engineer with a bachelor's degree from the [University of Science and Technology of China (中国科学技术大学, USTC)](https://www.ustc.edu.cn/). I work across **Research × Engineering × Entrepreneurship**, guided by a builder's approach: understand ideas deeply, turn them into reliable systems, and test them against real needs.

My current interests center on artificial intelligence, large language models, and the infrastructure that makes rapid experimentation possible. I am particularly interested in the space between a promising research result and a dependable product—the engineering decisions, evaluation loops, and iteration speed that determine whether an idea creates value.

> Good infrastructure compounds. It shortens the distance between an idea, an experiment, and something people can use.

I welcome conversations with researchers, engineers, and founders working on ambitious AI systems. [Get in touch](mailto:zhuzhenfang@ustc.edu) if there is a problem worth exploring together.
